ABOUT ME

Building reliable,
efficient AI systems.

I’m Weihang (Charles) Ding, a Machine Learning Engineer at GMI Cloud focused on model and agent evaluation. My work centers on the reliability, quality, and inference efficiency of large language models.

Previously, I researched LLM evaluation and judge bias at Stanford GSB’s AIM-Lab and worked on fine-tuning, knowledge distillation, and reinforcement learning workflows at Tencent. My academic background spans Analytics at UC Berkeley and Applied Mathematics with Information Computing Science at the University of Birmingham.

I’m interested in turning model capabilities into reliable systems: from post-training and evaluation to agent workflows and cost-aware model routing.
