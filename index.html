About Me

I’m Weihang (Charles) Ding, a Machine Learning Engineer at GMI Cloud, where I work on agent evaluation, semantic routing, and efficient inference. My academic background includes the Master of Analytics (Machine Learning) at UC Berkeley and Applied Mathematics with Information Computing Science at the University of Birmingham.

Previously, I was a research assistant at Stanford GSB’s AIM-Lab, studying LLM benchmarking and the reliability of LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation. At Tencent, as a forward deployed engineer, I worked on supervised fine-tuning, knowledge distillation, and reinforcement learning workflows for large language models. My earlier experience spans data science, quantitative research, and applied AI at Elsevier, LSQ Capital, CiaoAI, and Ant Group.

My interests center on agent evaluation, inference quality monitoring, efficient AI systems, human–AI interaction, and self-improving AI agents. My work runs from evaluation to loop engineering: I’m passionate about building the benchmarks and validators that show when an agent’s work can be trusted, and then closing the loop so that agents improve from that feedback.
