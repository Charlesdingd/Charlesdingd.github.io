About Me

I’m Weihang (Charles) Ding, a Machine Learning Engineer at GMI Cloud, where I work on model and agent evaluation, cost-aware model routing, and efficient inference. My academic background includes the Master of Analytics at UC Berkeley and Applied Mathematics with Information Computing Science at the University of Birmingham.

Previously, I was a student researcher at Stanford GSB’s AIM-Lab, studying LLM benchmarking and the reliability of LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation. At Tencent, I worked on supervised fine-tuning, knowledge distillation, and reinforcement learning workflows for large language models. My earlier experience spans data science, quantitative research, and applied AI at Elsevier, LSQ Capital, CiaoAI, and Ant Group.

My interests center on LLM and agent evaluation, post-training, and efficient AI systems. I’m particularly interested in understanding when models and agents work reliably, how to evaluate what they deliver, and how to balance quality with computational cost.
