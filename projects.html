alpha-evolve-loop

A self-improving alpha-mining loop for WorldQuant BRAIN. An LLM designer proposes fast-expression alphas, the platform’s simulator evaluates them, and the loop uses archived feedback to guide subsequent rounds.

  • Thompson sampling balances mutations of archived candidates with exploration of new expressions.
  • Validity checks, duplicate filtering, and ranking prioritize candidates before simulation.
  • Simulation verdicts and mutation lineage are preserved in an auditable archive.
