Selected Projects

Benchmarks, platforms, and research tools I have built or contributed to across agent evaluation, loop engineering, human-in-the-loop research, and quantitative research.

Human–AI Interaction · Full-stack

trustingdata.ai ↗

A platform for human-in-the-loop evaluation of multimodal and agentic AI, built at Stanford GSB’s AIM-Lab and deployed on Google Cloud. People label, score, and audit AI decisions, and every conclusion traces back to its evidence. It supports Stanford GSB and Wharton studies of human–LLM and human–agent interaction.

Stanford GSB AIM-Lab · Google Cloud

Agent Evaluation · Operations Research

Terminal-Bench Science task ↗

A benchmark task I authored for Terminal-Bench Science. An agent must plan admissions, rooms, and discharges for 450–500 patients across 70–80 beds under thirteen rules, estimate readmission-risk curves from confounded records, and hand in a branch-and-bound certificate within 1% of optimum that the verifier re-solves leaf by leaf. Frontier agents pass at most 2 of 4 runs.

Proposal approved · Domain review passed · Final review pending

Loop Engineering

VeriLoops

A loop-engineering system for model serving: an independent validator checks whether a deployment really works, and the loops built on top of it improve against that verdict.

Core contributor · Led by a former chief AI scientist at a Big Four hyperscaler · Internal at GMI Cloud · Closed source

AI Agents · Quantitative Research

quantdesk ↗

An agentic factor-mining and quant-strategy research stack for WorldQuant BRAIN. An LLM designer proposes alphas, the platform’s simulator evaluates them, and Thompson sampling over an auditable lineage archive decides what to mutate next. Admission gates refuse any statistic the sample cannot support.

Python · WorldQuant BRAIN Gold level

Agent Evaluation · Interactive Site

Trains but Doesn’t Learn ↗

The interactive companion to my EMNLP 2026 paper: a governed delivery-plane benchmark for LLM agents seated as forward-deployed engineers. The site walks through the ten delivery stages, the intake judgment switch, the trap taxonomy, and a CPU-replayable exhibit of a run that trains but doesn’t learn.

EMNLP 2026 · live demo

Agent Evaluation · Interactive Site

FDE-Bench ↗

The companion site to FDE-Bench: 136 deployment-configuration tasks across containerization, orchestration, and health monitoring. A ticket goes in, only declarative artifacts come out, and at submission the sandbox is destroyed and the agent’s files are re-applied to a pristine environment where hidden checks run. The site demonstrates the four-layer gated scoring lattice and the replay boundary.

NeurIPS 2026 AABA4ET workshop · live demo
